Sushank Jung Rawal

Personal ePortfolio | SARAS Labs

I worked as deputy project manager and frontend/UI/UX lead on SARAS, a workplace safety training application developed in response to Autoport Pvt. Ltd.'s brief. My focus was helping learners and trainers understand the system through navigation, responsive layouts, clear instructions, accessible controls, visual identity and presentations.

About my contribution

The project was completed collaboratively with Rijan Kapur Poudel, who led coordination, backend, data and core technical integration. The group's documented self/peer assessment assigns 40% of the overall contribution to my work and 60% to Rijan. Shared outcomes should not be interpreted as exclusive work by either person.

My main learning was that good UI work is not only visual polish. It also means reflecting saved-state accuracy, progress meaning, accessibility and user permissions correctly.

UI/UX case studies

Responsive design

Website and course interfaces had to work across screen sizes. I learned to check control order and clarity rather than only scale visual elements.

Accessibility

Sequencing interactions raised keyboard access concerns. This helped shift our thinking toward alternative controls and specific tests.

Clear progress and feedback

Client feedback showed the need to distinguish learning, assessment and practical completion. Frontend wording must match underlying data.

Brand and communication

Visual identity and presentation assets gave the application a coherent look and supported client communication.
